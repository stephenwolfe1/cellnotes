CellNotes

Notes made of cells, for Mac, iPhone and iPad.

Requires macOS 15 or later, or iOS 17 or later.

Download on the App Store Feedback

Every note in CellNotes is a stack of cells, and each cell has its own type. Write a paragraph in Markdown, add a snippet of code, follow it with an equation and a diagram, and each part is edited and displayed the way it should be.

Cells for every kind of note

Markdown and text

Headings, lists, links and tables, rendered as you write.

Code

Syntax highlighting for over 20 languages.

LaTeX math

Equations typeset properly, right in the note.

Diagrams

Flowcharts and sequence diagrams written in Mermaid.

Images

Paste a screenshot straight from the clipboard.

Checklists

Tick items off in the editor or the preview.

Link your notes

Type [[ in any cell and pick a note to link to it. Every note shows what links back to it under Linked from, so related ideas stay one click apart.

The graph view draws your whole library as a map of notes, links and tags. Open it from the toolbar on any device, or view just the neighborhood around the note you are reading.

Find anything

Press ⌘F to jump to any note or folder, or type #tag to filter by tag. On the Mac, CellNotes also reads the text in your images, so a screenshot of an error message or a photo of a whiteboard turns up in search. It happens on your Mac with Apple's Vision framework, nothing is uploaded, and a setting turns it off. Your notes also appear in Spotlight and Siri.

Works with Claude and other AI assistants

Mac only

CellNotes includes a built-in MCP server, so Claude and other assistants can work with your notes. Ask Claude what changed after an incident, which notes mention a topic, or to add a cell to today's note.

  • Lean by design: assistants start with a library overview and note outlines, read only the cells they need, make small edits to a single cell, and ask what changed since they last looked.
  • Finds notes by meaning: semantic search runs on your Mac, so an assistant can find the right note without reading your whole library.
  • Understands your links: assistants can follow links and backlinks, see related notes and read the graph.
  • Writes summaries: an assistant can add a short summary to a note, which shows under the note's title in Spotlight.
  • Folders, tags and checklists: list, search, read, create and edit notes, folders and tags, and tick checklist items.

Install it in Claude from Settings > Assistants. There is nothing else to download, and a switch turns it off whenever you like. The helper is sandboxed and talks only to the CellNotes app, never to your files directly, and CellNotes opens quietly in the background when an assistant needs it.

Shortcuts and Siri

CellNotes adds actions to the Shortcuts app on Mac, iPhone and iPad. Create notes, find them and append to them from your own shortcuts, or ask Siri.

On your Mac, iPhone and iPad

The same notes on every device, with an editor and preview made for each screen. CellNotes also fits the iPhone Duo and narrow iPad windows.

Your notes are your files

Notes are saved as plain files in a CellNotes folder in your iCloud Drive, so they sync across your devices and you can browse, back up or search them like any other files. Nothing is locked inside the app.

Private by design

No account, no tracking, no ads. CellNotes collects no data, and the assistant server stays on your Mac. Read the privacy policy.
